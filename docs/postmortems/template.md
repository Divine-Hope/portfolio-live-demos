# YYYY-MM-DD. What broke, in plain words

Status: Draft | Final

## Summary

Two or three sentences: what happened, for how long, and what users saw.

## Impact

What failed, for how long, and whether any data was lost or shown wrong. Numbers come from logs or metrics; say which.

## Timeline (UTC)

| Time | What happened |
|---|---|
| | |

## Cause

What actually went wrong, and why nothing caught it sooner.

## Fix

What changed, with links to the commits or PRs.

## What we changed so it can't recur

Alerts, tests, runbook steps, or design changes. Each one links to where it lives.
